FROM YOUR FIRST ORDER

Who needs FSSAI registration
or a food licence?

FSSAI is the Food Safety and Standards Authority of India. Food businesses need the appropriate registration or licence for their activity, applied for through its FoSCoS portal. Being small, home-based or delivery-only does not settle the category.

01

Home bakers & home cooks

Cakes, snacks, pickles or meals sold from home. Check the actual preparation and selling activity.

02

Cloud kitchens & tiffin services

Delivery-only kitchens, home-based canteens and dabba services need the right activity classification.

03

Cafés & restaurants

Map food service, turnover and every operating location before choosing the route.

04

Retailers & food shops

Grocery, packaged-food and other food retail activity must match the application.

05

Manufacturers & repackers

Product, processing and specialist food categories can change both the licence and document requirements.

06

Caterers, importers & platforms

Special categories need a separate eligibility review; the Basic offer is not a universal fit.

QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you start.

What does the ₹1,999 FSSAI package include?+

It covers assistance with one eligible new Basic Registration at one premises, document review, application preparation and coordination, plus the first-year government registration fee. No GST is added under the current RegisterBox pricing policy. State and Central licences require a separate quote.

Is this a one-year food licence?+

No. FSSAI registrations and licences issued on or after 1 April 2026 have perpetual validity unless suspended, cancelled or surrendered. The first-year description refers to the government fee included in this package. Later annual fees and ongoing compliance remain your responsibility; this is not an automatically renewing subscription.

Who can use Basic Registration in 2026?+

The revised general registration turnover band is up to ₹1.5 crore. Eligibility still depends on the official kind-of-business rules. A turnover figure alone is not enough: caterers, importers, e-commerce operators and other special activities can require a licence. We review your actual activity before confirming the Basic package.

Does a home baker or cloud kitchen need FSSAI?+

Selling food from home or operating only for delivery does not by itself remove the food registration or licensing requirement. Tell us what you make, where you prepare it and how you sell it so the correct FoSCoS activity can be selected.

Can I sell on Swiggy or Zomato after registration?+

An appropriate FSSAI registration or licence is one part of preparing a food business for platform onboarding. Each platform checks its own documents and operating requirements. RegisterBox does not guarantee listing, activation, orders or platform approval; marketplace onboarding is separate from this package.

Which documents do I need for Basic Registration?+

The official registration checklist asks for a photograph, government-issued photo ID and proof of the business activity address if it differs from the ID. Business name, food activity and premises details must be consistent. A licence route can require additional documents.

How long will my FSSAI application take?+

Timing depends on the correct category, complete documents, FoSCoS processing and any authority query or inspection. We confirm the preparation plan after reviewing your case. A payment or RegisterBox case reference does not establish government submission or approval.

What if I need a State or Central licence?+

We review the activity, premises and operating facts, then give you a separate written scope and quote. Government fees differ by route and may exceed the complete Basic package price. Do not purchase Basic solely because the business has low turnover.

Does one registration cover all my kitchens or outlets?+

Do not assume it does. Each premises and its activities must be reviewed, including any head-office or multi-State requirements. The ₹1,999 offer covers one eligible new Basic Registration at one premises.

What happens after my registration is issued?+

Verify the business name, address and activities on the official record. Keep annual government fees current, follow applicable hygiene and display duties, and assess changes in premises, activity or scale. Returns, label review, modifications and other compliance work are separate unless agreed in writing.

Can I apply without RegisterBox?+

Yes. You can apply directly on the official FoSCoS portal. RegisterBox is an independent private assistance provider, not FSSAI, a government portal or the authority issuing your certificate.

Are other local approvals included?+

No. FSSAI does not replace any applicable municipal trade permission, Shops and Establishments registration, GST, fire, premises or other activity-specific approval. We can discuss those separately after reviewing your business.
