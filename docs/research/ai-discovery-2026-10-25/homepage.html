Can I use my existing RegisterBox account?+

Yes. Sign in with the same email used in the app. The website uses the same account, business profiles, documents and backend services.

What happens when I start an application?+

You sign in, choose a business, answer relevant questions and upload supporting documents. Your progress is saved as a draft. Professional review and government submission are separate steps; a saved draft is not a filed application.

Can I manage more than one business?+

Yes. Switch between saved business profiles in your workspace. Applications, documents and GST workspaces remain linked to the selected business.

Can I pay or file GST returns here?+

You can choose a service package and pay securely through Razorpay Standard Checkout. The displayed RegisterBox service fee is the checkout total; no GST is added. Eligible nil GST filing has a ₹0 service fee. Payment does not file a return: GST filing still requires period and eligibility checks, your authorisation and the actual portal acknowledgement.

Are fees and government approvals guaranteed?+

No. Requirements, government fees and processing depend on the service and authority. Confirm the scope and quote with RegisterBox before proceeding. Government decisions cannot be guaranteed.

How do I get help?+

Call or WhatsApp +91 81477 77843, email registerboxindia@gmail.com, or request a callback. We have branches in New Thippasandra and Marathahalli, Bengaluru.
