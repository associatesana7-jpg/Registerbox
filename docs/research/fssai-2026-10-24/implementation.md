## Limits and next measurements

This is a source change, not a live deployment. The frontend export and the create/verify checkout and payment-journey functions consuming the shared catalogue/helper must be released together so Razorpay uses the advertised price. Existing created/pending payment orders keep their recorded amount and must not be silently repriced. No live payment or government filing was performed in QA.

More content does not establish a ranking or conversion improvement. Google does not require a special AI schema or llms file; FAQ markup does not promise a Google FAQ rich result. Track Search Console impressions, query coverage, clicks and valid enquiries after deployment; compare application-start and completion rates with a dated baseline. The audit did not establish current rankings, conversion rates or competitor customer-count claims.
