# RegisterBox FSSAI page — official research

Checked 24 October 2026. Primary sources only. The user confirmed ₹1,999 is for eligible Basic Registration, including the first annual government registration fee; State/Central are separately quoted.

## Commercial offer and correct wording

Recommended: **FSSAI Basic Registration — ₹1,999. Includes RegisterBox application assistance and the first year’s ₹100 government registration fee for one new registration at one premises. State and Central licences are quoted separately.**

Use “first-year package” / “first annual government fee included”, not “one-year licence”, “one-year validity”, or “renew every year”. New certificates issued on/after 1 April 2026 have perpetual validity. Ongoing annual government fees, applicable returns, hygiene obligations and modifications remain. Do not imply approval, platform onboarding, inspections, testing, local permits or future annual fees are included. Do not invent GST treatment or add “GST included” without the established checkout policy. Government fees are paid through official FoSCoS; RegisterBox’s assistance is a private service, not a government fee.

## Current eligibility, effective dates and exceptions

**13 March 2026 FSSAI turnover order**, effective **1 April 2026**: normal turnover bands are Registration up to ₹1.5 crore, State above ₹1.5 crore through ₹50 crore, Central above ₹50 crore. The order supersedes previous turnover/category criteria. Do not publish the obsolete ₹12 lakh / ₹20 crore thresholds or old capacity bands as current universal rules.

[Official turnover order](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2026/03/69b4054bb6cd6Order%20dated%2013thMarch2026_Revised%20Turnover%20threshold.pdf)

**FoSCoS category/fee table updated 1 April 2026**, pages 1–9: ordinary registrations ₹100/year; ordinary State licences ₹5,000/year; Central ₹7,500/year. Restaurant category includes delivery-only operations. Home-based canteens/dabba services can register through ₹1.5 crore. Special categories override general bands: caterers require State up to ₹50 crore; grain/cereal/pulse milling requires State regardless of turnover. Importers, ecommerce operators, proprietary foods, nutraceuticals/health supplements, non-specified foods, Ayurveda Aahara, radiation processing and specified export activities require Central regardless of turnover. Multi-state head offices and airport/seaport activities have Central requirements. Some railway/central-government categories have separate ₹2,000 fees; hawker/Anganwadi registration fees can be waived. Assess actual activity, premises and products before checkout. This means ₹1,999 cannot honestly be an all-category all-inclusive licence price.

[Current FoSCoS eligibility and annual fees](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/Revised_2ndApril2026KindofBusinessEligibility.pdf)

Capacity: current table uses turnover for ordinary dairy, oil, slaughter, meat, fish and general manufacturing, rather than the old litres/kg/animal-count bands. Higher-risk foods still have hygiene/testing requirements. Do not assume every manufacturer qualifies for Basic: special-product/KoB exceptions above remain.

## Validity, annual fees, migration and continuing duties

**FSSAI FAQ dated 27 March 2026**, Q2,4–7,9–15: thresholds govern new applications on/after 1 April; existing FBO migration is supported through FoSCoS. Perpetual validity means valid unless suspended/cancelled/surrendered, with no periodic renewal. Q12 confirms certificates issued on/after 1 April are perpetual. Annual fees remain and operators may prepay multiple years. Category migration due to changed thresholds has no modification fee; previous State payments can be adjusted to registration annual fees. Number stays unchanged; migration uses backend self-declarations. Existing-certificate modifications follow the existing procedure. Risk-based inspections and food-safety compliance continue.

Exact short quote (Q4, 16 words): “they will remain valid, unless suspended, cancelled or surrendered. FBOs are not required to renew their Licenses/ Registrations.”

[Official 2026 implementation FAQs](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2026/03/69c6a23234827order_27032026.pdf)

**Final Gazette notification dated 10 March 2026**, Gazette issue 11 March (digital publication identifier CG-DL-E-12032026-270872). Comes into force on publication; threshold order has separate 1 April start. Regulation 2.1.7 replaces old expiry/renewal provisions: nonpayment of annual fees or failure to submit applicable returns by due date deems registration/licence suspended. No food-business activity is allowed during suspension. Revocation follows payment or return submission with due penalty. Business closure must be notified and certificate surrendered within 30 days; paid fees are not refunded on surrender. Regulation 2.1.17 allows risk-based inspections and directed third-party audits at FBO cost. Instant registration is discretionary/enabling, conditional on documents/procedure; do not promise every application immediate approval.

[Final March 2026 Gazette — verified downloaded official PDF](https://www.fssai.gov.in/docs/whatsnew/gazette/69b2a2c804123Notification%20dt%2010.03.2026.pdf)

## Who needs help / home kitchens and online selling

Audience can include home cooks, tiffin/dabba businesses, cloud kitchens, bakeries, cafés, restaurants, food retailers, manufacturers and distributors. Describe assistance across categories, while reserving the ₹1,999 package for eligible Basic applications.

Marketplace distinction: a food seller on somebody else’s marketplace is not automatically the ecommerce operator. The seller still needs its eligible premises/activity registration or licence. Running one’s own ecommerce food platform also needs ecommerce licensing. Delivery-platform acceptance and their documents remain separate; FSSAI alone does not guarantee onboarding.

[Official FSSAI FAQs, December 2022, Q80 and ecommerce definition near Q117](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/FAQs_Licensing_Registration_21_12_2022.pdf)

The 18 March 2026 ONDC order confirms Central licensing for entities listing food businesses/products and facilitating online transactions; it divides buyer/seller app obligations effective 1 April. Do not confuse an ONDC seller-app provider with the kitchen merchant.

[Official ONDC/ecommerce obligations](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2026/03/69baca7e9787aCompliance%20Obligation%20ONDC%20Model.pdf)

## Documents

Basic registration’s national FoSCoS list: applicant photograph; government-issued photo ID (e.g. Aadhaar/PAN/voter ID); business-address proof if different from photo-ID address. Keep food activities/products, turnover, premises address and contact details ready. Do not require a company incorporation certificate, GST, machinery plan or water report from every Basic applicant.

[Official registration documents](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/DocumentsrequiredforRegistrationCertificate.pdf)

PAN must be captured for applications; a registration applicant without PAN may provide a non-possession declaration. This prevents wrongly saying PAN is always an absolute precondition for Basic.

[Official PAN order](https://www.fssai.gov.in/upload/advisories/2024/11/67346d14cfbd7FSSAI%20Order%20dated%2013-11-2024_Mandatory%20PAN%20Complinace.pdf)
[Official FSSAI monthly update confirming order and exception](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/monthly_update/2025/02/67ab19089c153MA_November_2024.pdf)

State/Central documents depend on KoB. Typical general set: responsible-person/director/partner details; government ID/address proof; possession of premises; constitution documents; Form IX nominee except proprietor. Food services may need ingredient-water analysis; manufacturers additionally layout, equipment/capacity, product list, unit photos and relevant technical/food-safety evidence. Use conditional language and the current FoSCoS checklist, not a universal fixed list.

[Official KoB document order, 19 March 2021](https://www.fssai.gov.in/docs/whatsnew/advisories-orders/60583d478e669Order_KOB_License_22_03_2021.pdf)
[Official FSO manual containing KoB checklist](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/knowledge_hub/16230060e42ededcc7dFSO_Manual_05_07_2021_.pdf)

Local requirements can differ. Example official West Bengal circular asks local-body enlistment/NOC for specified registration/licensing activities. Therefore promise a tailored checklist and check local permissions; do not say FSSAI replaces trade/fire/municipal/land-use permissions.

[FoSCoS-hosted West Bengal document circular](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/West_Bengal_New_doc_04_Nov_2024.PDF)

## Process and timing

1. Check real food activity/products, location, turnover and category.
2. Prepare applicable documents and review details with applicant.
3. Fill Form A (Registration) or Form B (Licence), submit documents and fee through FoSCoS.
4. Save reference and track application; answer authority queries and accommodate inspection if applicable.
5. Download issued certificate; check details, display as applicable and maintain annual fees/hygiene duties.

[Official FoSCoS application guide](https://foscos.fssai.gov.in/assets/docs/HowtoapplyforLicense_registrationcertificate..pdf)

Standard regulatory framework: registration action within seven days; if inspection is ordered, process may extend to 30 days. Licence within 60 days of complete application; deficiencies/inspection responses affect real timeline. 2026 permits instant registration under specified procedure; Tatkal remains category/verification dependent. Avoid guaranteed 24/48-hour approval or presenting RegisterBox preparation time as authority issuance time.

[Official FSSAI timing FAQ, Q57–58](https://fssai.gov.in/upload/uploadfiles/files/FAQs_Licensing_Registration_21_06_2021.pdf)
