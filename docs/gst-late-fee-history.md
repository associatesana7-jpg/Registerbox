# GST pending-return history worksheet

Reviewed: 29 October 2026. The worksheet is browser-only and does not fetch portal records or submit returns.

The user example is last GSTR-3B tax period October 2018 and suo motu cancellation in 2022. The example button assumes registration on 1 July 2017 and both cancellation dates on 31 October 2022, explicitly labels those as assumptions, and leaves GSTR-1 and later QRMP history unknown. It never converts the statement “last filed October 2018” into a quarterly GSTR-3B history.

The dates wizard distinguishes registration effective date, calculation date, cancellation effective date and order date. GSTR-1 and GSTR-3B are separate. The older GSTR-1 monthly/quarterly choice applies only before January 2021. From January 2021, both forms are monthly until a verified QRMP start makes both quarterly, following rules 59 and 61 as substituted by Notification 82/2020. An earlier quarterly GSTR-1 arrangement never carries forward with monthly GSTR-3B after December 2020. Last-filed period validation uses the regime actually applicable to that period: a post-2020 monthly GSTR-1 may end in February, even if GSTR-1 was quarterly before 2021. At most one QRMP change is supported; unknown history produces an explicitly draft checklist and blocks numeric fee output. Active open periods are omitted. Cancellation partial quarters require period confirmation.

The engine generates possible returns through effective cancellation, and a GSTR-10 row unless already filed. GSTR-10 base due date is three calendar months after the later cancellation/order date, with month-end clamping. Other historic due dates are deliberately not fabricated. Every entered due date must be after the return period and separately confirmed against the applicable notification. GSTR-10 cannot be assigned a due date earlier than its statutory base.

Normal fee uses pre-June 2021 combined ceiling ₹10,000, then nil ₹500 / non-nil ₹2,000, ₹5,000 or ₹10,000 by preceding-year aggregate turnover. Daily figures are combined central and State/UT: eligible nil ₹20, other ₹50; GSTR-10 ₹200 capped at ₹10,000. Matching State/UT provisions, applicable relief and portal entries require review. July–September 2017 and partial April/May 2021 QRMP fee transition are not numerically asserted. A possible concession blocks a numeric result. Older return three-year restrictions are flagged separately; standard fee is never called final payable or permission to file. Tax, payment interest, penalties, demands and cancellation stock/ITC adjustments are excluded.

The CSV contains only fixed labels and validated amounts/dates. It retains uncalculated rows and reasons. No GSTIN, contact detail or client identifier is requested, persisted or sent to a third party. Source links in `HISTORY_SOURCES` are rendered in the static page alongside FAQs and scope.

Regression tests: `node --test scripts/test-gst-late-fee-history.mjs`. The fully specified example with no QRMP now has 80 rows: 48 monthly GSTR-3B, 9 quarterly GSTR-1 through December 2020, 22 monthly GSTR-1 from January 2021 through cancellation in October 2022, and one final return. The verified January 2021 QRMP variant remains 52 rows. Tests also cover independent pre-2021 frequency, later QRMP starts, last-filed validation under each regime, unknown histories, the 29 October review cutoff, partial periods, leap years, cap regimes, due-date boundaries, relief, filing restriction and amounts already paid.
