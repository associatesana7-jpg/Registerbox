## Topics and product claims

- GSTR-3B filing process: private preparation and guided NIL filing. Read-only production audit found acknowledged NIL filings; no regular GSTR-3B success was verified. The article explains eligibility, signatory OTP, final approval and acknowledgement. No unattended one-click or measured speed promise.
- DIR-3 KYC: revised three-year framework verified against the Ministry of Corporate Affairs' 1 January 2026 release and linked Gazette notification. Dedicated filing is not present in RegisterBox: enquiry/call plus official portal buttons only.
- ESIC employers: current-code coverage, workforce records and official route selection. RegisterBox enquiry is not automatic ESIC registration.
- Shops and establishments: Karnataka-only e-Karmika procedure, separate from the existing preparation guide. No numeric fees published because official homepage and older SOP differ.

Each guide has its own source list, contextual citations, FAQ answers, source-check/publication date, guide jump button, direct call button, relevant service/enquiry/application links and reciprocal internal related-guide links.
