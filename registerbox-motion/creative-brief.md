# RegisterBox — Nil, made simple

32-second vertical product film, 1080 × 1920, 30 fps. English narration with an Indian English voice and an original light electronic music bed.

Reference: the supplied 42.675-second screen recording. It shows GSTR-3B period selection, nil conditions, declaration, signatory PAN, authorization, and an OTP screen. It does not show a completed filing or receipt. Reconstructed UI uses demo identifiers and condensed copy; no success receipt is invented.

## Direction

Use the app's navy, electric blue, pale surfaces, rounded cards, and green confirmation accents. Large editorial titles introduce one action at a time. Enlarge the relevant app UI instead of fitting tiny text into a phone. Small tap rings cause visible state changes. Cards share a stable position across the walkthrough. End on the brand and the filing action, not an invented result.

## Motion and audio

Editorial cubic easing for card entrances, critically damped springs for checkmarks and tap responses, 12-frame text stagger, 18-frame reveals, restrained focal scale. No arbitrary object drift. Original 100 BPM plucks and soft rhythm, quieter beneath narration, with intro and outro fades. Individual voice clips remain independently editable.
