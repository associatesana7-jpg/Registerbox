## Product evidence

The current customer service catalogue and application categories include company, LLP, GST registration, food/FSSAI and shops. Trademark exists in demo recommendations and the client-register service enum, but has no dedicated customer application category or public service route. The film must not depict a working trademark submission screen or promise automatic registration.

## Audio

Use Hindi-capable narration with English business terms pronounced naturally. Hindi portions should be supplied in Devanagari for speech synthesis. A human performance requires a supplied recording; a generated voice must be disclosed as synthetic. Do not reuse the previous American-English voice for Hinglish without checking pronunciation.
