# Brag Plan: RegisterBox

## What is this app?
RegisterBox is a mobile-first business workspace for discovering, preparing, and tracking Indian business registrations, documents, GST work, and compliance steps.

## The angle
Big ambitions should not create bigger paperwork. The film turns RegisterBox's real flow into a calm, confident progression: choose the next step, prepare the details, get practical guidance, and keep every document connected to the business.

## Hook (first 2-3 seconds)
"Big ambitions. Less paperwork." arrives over RegisterBox blue while a real product screen rises into view.

## Key moments (the middle)
- The actual home screen establishes the choice of GST filing, compliance review, registration, or starting a business.
- The food-business application screen shows relevant questions, a visible step count, and a saved draft flow.
- The actual AI workspace and Document Wallet screens show guidance, GST actions, and reusable business records.

## Outro / punchline
RegisterBox lands at full scale with the source-site line: "Built for the way India does business."

## User flow worth showing
Choose a next step -> prepare an application with relevant questions -> use AI guidance and keep documents in one private wallet.

## Tone
- Preset: app-store
- Creative direction: confident Indian business companion; polished, clear, and practical
- Interpretation: Smooth transitions, clean feature-led writing, genuine product screens, restrained motion, and no exaggerated claims.

## Format: landscape — 1920x1080
## Duration: 20 seconds

## Visual identity (from the project)
- Background: #071946
- Accent: #0865F7
- Text: #FFFFFF on dark; #0A1943 on light
- Display font: Archivo Black
- Body font: Space Mono / Inter-compatible system UI within screenshots
- Strongest visual element: the cobalt campaign artwork framing the real RegisterBox mobile interface

## Share copy (draft)
RegisterBox brings applications, documents, GST workflows and practical AI guidance into one business workspace—built for the way India does business.

## Audio direction
- Role: warm, polished product bed with natural narration
- Music: happy-beats-business-moves-vol-1-by-ende-dot-app.mp3
- Music treatment: start immediately at low volume, carved beneath voice, then lift slightly for the final brand lockup
- Music cue guidance: bundled 120.19 BPM preset; use beat windows around 3.02-6.03s for the screen sequence and the 17.02s strong cue for the brand close
- Audio-reactive treatment: subtle; the blue glow and device depth may breathe a few percent with music energy
- SFX posture: sparse and motion-matched
- Audio-coupled moments: screen advances on the half-second beat grid; soft click on the application action; warm impact at the 17.02s logo reveal
- Restraint rule: voice remains primary; no synthetic announcer cadence, heavy pulses, or sharp repeated clicks

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Big ambitions — 3.5s
The RegisterBox navy/cobalt world opens with "Big ambitions. Less paperwork." and the real home screen sliding up at camera-right.
Sequential/interaction: headline resolves in two beats, then the phone settles.
Audio intent: warm human voice opens immediately; music establishes confidence.
Audio-coupled idea: headline and device settle on nearby beat points.
Music: upbeat, polished business bed.
Transition mood: smooth push -> Scene 2

### Scene 2 — Choose and prepare — 4.5s
Home and application screens ride left as the application screen becomes primary. Copy: "Choose your next step." then "Prepare the details."
Sequential/interaction: yes — screens advance one by one and the application progress line fills subtly.
Audio intent: light UI motion support beneath narration.
Audio-coupled idea: screen advance and one button click.
Transition mood: clean push -> Scene 3

### Scene 3 — Practical guidance — 4.5s
The actual AI workspace becomes the hero. Copy: "Practical AI guidance." A small label reinforces that guidance supports decisions and review.
Sequential/interaction: the AI screen advances into focus; a consent/check accent locks in.
Audio intent: music stays tucked beneath the voice.
Audio-coupled idea: one soft selection click.
Transition mood: smooth push -> Scene 4

### Scene 4 — Everything connected — 4.5s
The actual Document Wallet screen becomes primary. Copy: "Your documents. One private wallet." The frame shows the real saved/verified document counts and provider options using fictional sample context.
Sequential/interaction: the document screen catches into place and the two stat tiles highlight in sequence.
Audio intent: a gentle rise toward the close.
Audio-coupled idea: two restrained card accents spaced for readability.
Transition mood: soft blur crossfade -> Scene 5

### Scene 5 — Brand close — 3s
RegisterBox wordmark resolves over navy with a yellow brand star and the line "Built for the way India does business."
Sequential/interaction: letters cascade, the star lands last, then the frame holds.
Audio intent: music opens slightly after the narration, with one warm logo impact.
Audio-coupled idea: logo reveal at the 17.02s strong cue.
Music: confident final hold and short fade.

**Music mood for this video:** upbeat, polished, modern Indian business
**Audio summary:** Natural narration leads; music and a few warm interface accents support the real product flow and lift into the final brand lockup.
