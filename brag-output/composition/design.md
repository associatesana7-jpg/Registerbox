# RegisterBox Frame Design

## Overview
A confident, practical business companion. The visual world comes directly from RegisterBox: cobalt blue, deep navy, pale ice surfaces, a small yellow star, rounded cards, and crisp trustworthy information hierarchy.

## Composition Rules
- Use the exact brand colors above.
- Keep product screenshots recognizable and uncropped inside tall device frames.
- Anchor large copy to the left and product UI to the right or center.
- Use saturated cobalt as a focal color and navy as the stable field.
- Use the yellow star only as a small payoff accent.

## What NOT to Do
- No purple gradients, neon cyber styling, generic particle fields, or government-emblem imagery.
- No invented metrics, approvals, filings, or verification claims.
- No dense card grids competing with the real product screens.
- No aggressive bounces, glitch effects, or robotic voice styling.
